BFSI – Case Study 1
Modernization of Legacy Core Banking Apps for One of the Top U.S. Banks

Industry: BFSI

Problem

Bank was facing heavy operational burden due to legacy COBOL-based systems spread across 120+ branches.
Key pain areas:

Slow account onboarding (avg 9 days)

High compliance failures (>18 findings per audit)

Systems not cloud scalable

Solution Delivered

✔ Cloud native modernization using Azure App Services
✔ Claims processing workflow moved to microservices
✔ Automated control checks with AI models
✔ Data warehouse modernization to Synapse

Business Outcomes

🔹 Reduced onboarding time from 9 days → <3 days
🔹 Audit failures reduced by 72%
🔹 Yearly infrastructure cost reduced by $1.8M
